Put your problem-solving skills to the test in our **Competitive Programming Arena**. Solve complex algorithmic challenges under strict time constraints.

### Rules
* **Team Size**: Individual participation only.
* **Platform**: The contest will be hosted on HackerRank / Codeforces (link provided at event start).
* **Languages**: C++, Java, Python, and Go are allowed.
* **Plagiarism**: Any form of copying or discussion will lead to immediate disqualification. Plagiarism detection tools will be run.
* **Scoring**: Ranked by number of solved problems, with ties broken by time-to-solve and penalty points.
